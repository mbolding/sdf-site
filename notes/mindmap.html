Mind map

Double-click empty space for a new idea, joined to the one you had selected; double-click an idea to rename it. Drag ideas to move them and the background to look around; pinch, Ctrl-scroll or the buttons zoom. Shift-click, or Join, links two ideas or unlinks them. Keys: Tab new idea, Enter rename, Delete remove, arrows to move between ideas. The map is saved in this browser as you go.
