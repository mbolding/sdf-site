Notes

Three small tools for thinking things through. Everything stays in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Notepad

Jot notes down, then edit, pin, search and copy them. Download them as text or back them up.

Mind map

Ideas as bubbles joined by lines. Drag them about, works with a finger too, saves as you go, and exports to a PNG or to Mermaid.

Mermaid diagrams

Type flowcharts, sequence diagrams, Gantt charts and more as text and watch them draw. Examples to start from, and SVG or PNG to save.
